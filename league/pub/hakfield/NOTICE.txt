The release combines public recorded play, town-dependent routes, and
hakdevelopment's control and market integration. Distribution changes:
comments/research notes removed, local names shortened, source compressed,
and a reproducible notebook and match diagnostics added.

Town-dependent route lineage: Ahmed Berat Ozer's Kaggriculture V43 and
yhay81's shop-router series. Retained upstream credits: thomastschinkel,
yhay81, destbreso, aurax7, tetsutani, prvsiyan, Dmitrii Gluzdov, and
Rayk Kretzschmar. Public recorded play supplied the base route.
